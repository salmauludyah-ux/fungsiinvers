Formulasi Matematis & Rumus Cepat

1. Fungsi Linear
$f(x) = ax + b, \quad a \neq 0$
$f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x - b}{a}$
2. Fungsi Rasional
$f(x) = \dfrac{ax + b}{cx + d}, \quad x \neq -\dfrac{d}{c}$
$f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{-dx + b}{cx - a}, \quad x \neq \dfrac{a}{c}$
